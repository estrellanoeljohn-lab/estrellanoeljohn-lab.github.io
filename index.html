RepTrail

A gym progress tracker that answers one question: am I on track for my goal?

My friends started going to the gym and couldn’t tell if they were making progress. RepTrail turns their weight, measurements, and lifts into one clear answer.

Key Features

  • 7-day trend and a weekly pace check against your goal
  • Then vs now comparison of weight and measurements
  • Lift log with estimated 1RM and PR detection

Tech Stack

  • Flutter
  • Firebase Auth
  • Firestore

StudyPlanner

A study planner that keeps courses, deadlines, and study time in one place.

Students juggle many courses and deadlines, and work piles up at the last minute. StudyPlanner puts tasks, reminders, and a focus timer in one view.

  • Tasks with priorities, notes, and in-app reminders
  • Focus timer (Pomodoro) that logs sessions per course
